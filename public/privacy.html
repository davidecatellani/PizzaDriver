Pizza Driver – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Pizza Driver is a game by Davide Catellani. This page explains what data the game uses.

What we collect

  • Leaderboard entries. When you finish a delivery, the game sends the rider name you typed, your tips and your delivery times to our world leaderboard, together with a random anonymous ID created on your device. The name is shown publicly on the leaderboard. Do not use your real name if you prefer not to.
  • Game progress (money, garage, settings) is stored only on your device and never leaves it.

We do not collect your email, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising ID, and we do not sell or share data with third parties. The game shows no ads in this version.

Where the data lives

The leaderboard runs on Cloudflare Workers. Standard server logs (such as IP addresses) may be processed by Cloudflare to deliver the service.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes everything stored on your device. To remove your name from the leaderboard, email davidecatellani@gmail.com.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
